# OUTREACH — venues, templates, safety

## 1. Venues (start with these, they're reachable at 13+)

### Discord marketplaces (strongest for you — Discord-native, no age problem)
| Venue | Link / how to find | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Freelance Marketplace** (~7.5k members) | `discord.com/invite/freelance-marketplace-1002522561613135892` | Dedicated channels for selling dev services; post in their **forum** channels per format |
| **FreelanZo** | `discord.com/invite/freelanzo-hire-jobs-freelance-1237622676449792073` | Hiring/job-feed channels |
| **Discord.do listings** | `discord.do/freelance-marketplace/` | Server directory + their linked community |
| **DISBOARD `freelance` tag** | `disboard.org/servers/tag/freelance` | Browse & pick the 3 most active |
| Bot-dev communities | search DISBOARD for "bot development" | Often have `#showcase` + `#help` — help people *publicly* for free first; DMs follow |

**How to join properly:** read each server's rules channel *before* posting. Post the format they
ask for (most require a specific ad format in a forum channel). One good ad > ten copy-pastes.

### Reddit (check current rules at posting time — you're the one posting)
- `r/Discord_Bots` — big audience of server owners + bot devs. Build/showcase posts usually welcome; hiring/ads often restricted → **read the sidebar rules first**
- `r/forhire` — `[Hiring]` posts you can reply to (their sub has strict title/format rules)
- `r/slavelabour` — small paid tasks, PayPal-based; good for first paid job
- `r/SideProject`, `r/InternetIsBeautiful` — for the build-in-public/portfolio side (`POSTS.md`)
- Never repost the same ad across subs the same day (spam filters).

### Direct outreach (zero gatekeepers)
1. Find servers where people publicly ask for bot help: Discord support servers, r/Discord_Bots comment threads, servers with a `#bot-requests`/`#suggestions` channel.
2. Help **free, publicly, thoroughly** for 2–3 messages → then offer to build the bigger thing.
3. Server owners with money (gaming clans, esports orgs, trading communities) post in `#suggestions` wishing for features. That wish = your quote.

### NOT worth your time
- Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal, Contra, 99designs → **18+**, enforced at payout
- Any site asking you to pay to get work (job scams)
- "Pay for promotion" servers

## 3. DM reply flow (keep it short)
1. "What do you need it to do, in one sentence?" + "How big is your server?"
2. 2–3 clarifying questions max (see `OFFER.md` scoping list)
3. Fixed price + turnaround + what's excluded → "Want me to start?"
4. Only then discuss payment (`PAYMENTS.md`).

## 4. ⚠️ SAFETY RULES — read twice, they exist to protect you

**Never:**
- ❌ Share your Discord token, Cloudflare login, or `admin-secrets.txt` — nobody legit ever needs them
- ❌ Pay money to "get a job," "verify," or "unlock escrow" — that's a scam, 100% of the time
- ❌ Give out your home address, school, phone number, or real full name
- ❌ Accept a "job" that asks you to message people spam-style or impersonate anyone
- ❌ Let a stranger install something on your machine or run their scripts
- ❌ Continue talking to anyone who gets angry, pushes fast, or offers way above your price

**Always:**
- ✅ Keep first deals small and public (in the marketplace's own channels when possible)
- ✅ Screenshot agreements before starting work
- ✅ Get paid before handing over source code or the bot token for delivery
- ✅ Walk away from any deal that feels off — a lost $30 is cheaper than a stolen account
- ✅ If someone offers payment in gift cards/crypto only: walk.

**Impersonation scam warning:** a very common Discord scam is "I'll pay you, now click this link /
log in with Discord here / scan this QR" — those steal *your* account. Real payment = a normal
PayPal link or invoice, never a "verify your account" page.

**Scripts for awkward moments:**
- Pushy: "I don't do that — happy to scope the actual job though."
- Wants free work: "I can do a small paid first task so you can see the quality."
- Wants you on Upwork: "That platform requires 18+, so I invoice directly via PayPal instead."
